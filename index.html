How I work

Process

From raw data to a result you can rely on, in three steps.

1. Share your raw data

Spreadsheets, scans, forms, website exports or supplier sources — any format works. I confirm exactly what I need and how the result will be delivered.

2. I clean, mine & verify

Entry, de-duplication, format standardisation and cross-checking every value against its source, so accuracy is built in rather than assumed.

3. Receive ready-to-use data

Clean, structured, verified data in your preferred format — on schedule, with your information kept confidential throughout.

What I do

Services

Accurate, verified data work for businesses that depend on their records.

Data entry

Production, inventory, order and customer records entered accurately into Excel, Google Sheets, Word or Oracle — plus manual typing and PDF-to-Excel extraction at high volumes, near-zero errors.
